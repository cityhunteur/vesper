# Code of conduct

Vesper follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good faith, and focus on the work. Harassment, personal attacks
and discriminatory language are not accepted in issues, pull requests, reviews or any other project space.

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer through
[GitHub](https://github.com/cityhunteur). Reports are handled in confidence. The maintainer may remove content,
and may warn, suspend or ban a participant, as the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines describe.
